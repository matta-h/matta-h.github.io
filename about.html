ABOUT / MATTHEW HUYNH

Engineering with
a hands-on perspective.

I’m a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of California, Merced, with a minor in Materials Science & Engineering.

Connected disciplines.
Practical development.

My interests center on electromechanical product development: bringing mechanical design, sensing, and controls together in working systems.

My project work spans mechanism design, UAV development, sensor integration, robotics, and simulation. I’m interested in the details of individual components and how those components work together.

AREAS OF INTEREST

  • Mechanical design & prototyping
  • Robotics & controls
  • Sensing systems
  • Simulation
